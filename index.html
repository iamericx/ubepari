OUR STORY

Our Story

Erick Started It, We Perfect It, You Benefit.

Our journey began with Erick, a Doctor of Medicine with a vision beyond the white coat. While the world expected him to follow a traditional path in healthcare, he chose to pursue his passion for technology and entrepreneurship. What started as a small laptop business in 2020 while still in university has grown into Ubepari PC, a company dedicated to providing high-performance computers, empowering customers, and inspiring the tech industry in Tanzania and beyond.

OUR MISSION

Our Mission

At Ubepari PC, our mission is simple: to empower our customers with reliable, high-performance technology, inspire innovation in the tech industry, and create a culture where our team can thrive. We are committed to delivering computers and solutions that make a difference in people’s lives, whether for work, study, or creativity.
